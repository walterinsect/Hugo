**Name:** walterinsect  
Using this name since I first entered this cyberworld back in...2005? All of my cyber presence is unified under this name. Recently I made some connecting points between this and my legal name, but under this name, I'm "almost real, but not quite".

**This blog:**  
This blog is a personal archive of walterinsect, to record thoughts and process at certain point in time, and this blog act as storage space for that purpose. Twitter account is used in the same way. Twitter records raw data of thoughts, whereas this blog act as storage for more organized, discrete thoughts with some logical coherence. Thus it is likely that it is worthless and not interesting for anybody. Unless you are dying of lack of useless texts to read, this is not a place for you.

I believe in outsourcing my thoughts and memory in an accessible way for future reference. In effect I record all the "shameful regrettable past" and through that I can  record everything I thought at certain point in my life. I do this with clear objectives, and thus I am not trying to justify my severe case of Social Media addiction. No, really.
